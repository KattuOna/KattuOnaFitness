A shop that isn't selling you out

KattuOna has a Shop tab for gear and supplements — built on the same no-tracking rule as everything else in the app.

Picked from your own logs

Turn on "Personalize Shop" and it reorders products using workouts and meals already on your phone — your goal, your soreness, your history. Off by default, and nothing about it is ever sent anywhere.

You check out elsewhere

Tap a product and it opens the seller's own page in your browser. KattuOna never sees or handles your payment details — it's just a link.

No account, no checkout markup
