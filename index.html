Built to be genuinely simple for someone just starting out, without cutting the features people who've trained for years actually want.

Workouts & programs

Log any workout, follow a structured program, or let KattuOna build one around your recovery and schedule — calisthenics and home workouts included.

🍽️

Nutrition & diet

Log food by hand, by barcode, or by photo. The AI Chef can turn whatever's in your fridge into recipe ideas that actually hit your calorie and protein targets.

🤖

Kattu, your AI coach

Ask questions, get meal and training ideas, and check in on readiness — powered by a provider you choose, or a model you run yourself.

Sleep & recovery

Automatic sleep detection and a daily readiness check feed straight into training recommendations, inspired by published recovery research.

Track what you actually take — brand, dose, ingredients — with reminders, scanned by barcode or described to the AI in a sentence.
