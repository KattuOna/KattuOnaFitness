One app, not six subscriptions

Built to be genuinely simple for someone just starting out, without cutting the features people who've trained for years actually want.

Workouts & programs

Log any session, follow a structured program, or let KattuOna build one around your recovery and schedule — calisthenics and home workouts included, not bolted on.

Nutrition

Log by hand, barcode, or photo. The AI Chef turns what's in your fridge into recipes that actually hit your calorie and protein targets.

Kattu, your AI coach

Ask questions, get meal and training ideas, check in on readiness — powered by a provider you choose, or a model you run yourself.

Sleep & recovery

Automatic sleep detection and a daily readiness check feed straight into what KattuOna recommends for training.

Supplements

Track what you actually take — brand, dose, ingredients — with reminders, scanned by barcode or described to the AI.

Your data, your call

Backups go wherever you choose — your own server, your own Drive, or nowhere at all. Never to the developer.
